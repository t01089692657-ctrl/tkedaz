## 二、激活话术库（13场景，老客户激活直接引用；每场景3轮递进）

> 每条含：触发条件 → 3轮话术要点 → 回复判断 → 升级条件 → 降级/归档条件 → 标签。英文话术为公司原文可直接发送。

**1. 通用未回复（A/B/C）**
- R1: Hi, just checking if you received my message. May I know which model you are interested in and how many units you need?
- R2: Different models are suitable for different uses. Is your project for personal use, Airbnb, resort/campsite, or resale?
- R3: If you are still interested, you can send me your country and quantity. I will recommend the most suitable model for you.
- 判断：回国家/用途/数量→继续筛；仍只问价→C；不回→按等级降。升级：补齐国家+用途+数量→B；愿视频/报价→A。归档：连续3次不回→C归档；只问最低价不补资料→D。标签：待补资料/已归档

**2. 问价后消失（B/C）**
- R1: May I know your destination country and quantity? Then I can give you a more accurate price.
- R2: The final cost depends on model, quantity, shipping, and configuration. Do you want a low-cost model or a better model for rental business?
- R3: I can send you a suitable price range first. Please tell me your country and how many units you plan to buy.
- 升级：接受总成本概念→B；有明确采购时间→A。降级：只要最低价不说国家数量→C/D。标签：待补资料/预算不足

**3. 已报价未回复（A/B）**
- R1: Have you checked the quotation? May I know if the model and price are suitable for your project?
- R2: If the budget is high, we can also recommend a more cost-effective model or adjust the configuration for you.
- R3: Should I keep following this project for you, or is it postponed for now?
- 判断：说价格高→可调型号；在考虑→B；问付款/交期→A。升级：问付款/交期/装柜/样板→A并交销售。降级：7天不回→降B；14-30天→长期维护。标签：已报价/预算不足/待付款

**4. 代理商未回复（A/B）**
- R1: May I know if you already sell prefab houses, tiny homes, or similar products in your local market?
- R2: If you have a showroom, customer base, or installation team, we can recommend models suitable for your market.
- R3: We usually start distributor cooperation with 1 sample unit first. Would you consider a sample order to test your market?
- 升级：提供公司主页/展厅/安装团队/样板计划→A。降级：不买样板却要独家→D；只要低价表→C。标签：☆代理/待样板/待视频

**5. 民宿/营地项目未回复（A/B）**
- R1: For resort or campsite projects, we usually recommend models based on land, quantity, and opening schedule.
- R2: If you can share your project location and expected number of units, we can suggest a better product combination.
- R3: Do you already have land or a project site? We can recommend T series, A-frame, or capsule models based on your plan.
- 升级：提供土地图片/数量/开业时间→A。降级：无土地无预算→C；不给项目资料→C/D。标签：☆项目/待视频/待报价

**6. 个人业主未回复（B/C）**
- R1: Do you already have land or space for installation? This will help us recommend the right model.
- R2: For personal use, shipping, local installation, and permits are important. Do you have a local installer or builder?
- R3: If one unit shipping cost is high, we can recommend models with better loading and lower total cost.
- 升级：有土地有预算能接受进口安装→B/A。降级：无土地无预算→C；要求包全部落地→D。标签：有土地/有安装/待补资料

**7. 华人/中间人未回复（B/C）**
- R1: 您好，方便确认一下您是项目方本人，还是帮终端客户找产品？
- R2: 如果是帮客户找，麻烦确认项目国家、预计数量、客户预算和采购时间。
- R3: 如果项目比较明确，可以安排我们和终端客户一起沟通，方案和报价会更准确。
- 升级：能安排终端会议→A；提供完整项目资料→B/A。降级：不能说终端只要资料→C/D。标签：华人/中间/待补资料

**8. 美加澳认证客户未回复（B/C/D）**
- R1: Local approval depends on your city, land use, foundation, and local building code. Do you have a local builder or certifier?
- R2: We can provide product documents, drawings, and support some material/configuration standards, but full local approval needs local review.
- R3: If you have a local builder or engineer, we can provide documents for them to check first.
- 升级：客户自己处理审批我们供资料→B/A。降级：要求保证整屋认证/政府审批→D。标签：认证风险/有安装

**9. 嫌运费贵未回复（B/C）**
- R1: Shipping cost is an important part of the total cost. May I know if you are considering 1 unit or more units?
- R2: For some models, ordering more units can reduce the average shipping cost per unit.
- R3: If the total cost is higher than your budget, we can recommend a more cost-effective model.
- 升级：愿调型号/数量→B；有批量→A。降级：听到运费就不接受→C/D。标签：预算不足/待补资料

**10. 看资料不回复（B/C）**
- R1: Have you checked the models? Which one do you like more: T series, expandable house, A-frame, or capsule house?
- R2: If you tell me your usage and quantity, I can help recommend the most suitable model.
- R3: Do you prefer low-cost models or more attractive models for rental business?
- 升级：明确目标产品+数量→B；项目客户→A。归档：只收藏资料不回复→7天后低频维护。标签：待补资料

**11. 报价后说太贵未回复（B/C）**
- R1: I understand. May I know which part is higher than your budget: product price, shipping cost, or total landed cost?
- R2: We can recommend a more cost-effective model if your budget is limited.
- R3: What is your target budget per unit? I can check if there is a suitable model.
- 升级：给目标预算且愿换型号→B。降级：只要求降价不说预算→C/D。标签：预算不足/待报价

**12. 样板意向未回复（A/B）**
- R1: Sample order is a good start for testing your market. Which model do you want to test first?
- R2: For sample orders, we can help recommend models easier to sell and install in your market.
- R3: If you confirm the model and destination, our sales manager can check the sample quotation for you.
- 升级：确认样板型号+国家+时间→A，立即交销售核价。降级：样板只是嘴上说不回细节→B/C。标签：待样板/☆代理

**13. 视频会议意向未定时间（A）**
- R1: Would you like to arrange a short video meeting this week to confirm the model and project details?
- R2: Please let me know your available time and time zone. Our sales manager can join the meeting.
- R3: If video meeting is not convenient now, you can send project details first, and we will prepare suggestions.
- 升级：确定会议时间→A重点跟进。降级：多次不定时间也不发资料→B/C。标签：待视频/☆项目/☆代理

**中文轻激活模板（通用，原文）**：你好，我的朋友，最近一切顺利吗？我看到你没回复我的消息，你还需要我们的产品吗？我们马上上线四个新产品，您如果还感兴趣，可以留言，我会第一时间和您同步信息。
