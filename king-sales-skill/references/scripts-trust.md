# 移动房屋外贸销售 AI 参考：话术库 · 金句库 · 信任故事库

> 使用总则：先判断客户，再发话术；不机械应答；每句话服务于下一步动作。客户问价→先判断要总价还是出厂价；说贵→先判断是预算不够还是价值没讲清；不回复→先判断没兴趣还是没到决策时间；想代理→先判断有无公司、样品、展厅、渠道和来中国意愿。

## 一、全场景中英文话术库

### 1. 开场与客户判断

**新客户刚加上**
- 中：您好，很高兴认识您。我是中国源头工厂，主要生产模块化房屋、拓展屋、民宿房和度假屋。您是想自己使用，还是用于民宿/项目呢？我可以根据您的用途推荐更适合的型号。
- EN: Hi, nice to meet you. We are a source factory in China, mainly producing modular houses, expandable houses, homestay cabins, and vacation houses. Are you planning to use it for yourself, or for a homestay/project? I can recommend a more suitable model based on your usage.

**客户只发了"Hi"**
- 中：您好，请问您是想了解哪一类房屋？比如自住小屋、民宿房、拓展屋，还是想做项目/代理合作？
- EN: Hi, may I know what kind of house you are interested in? For example, personal living cabin, homestay house, expandable house, or project/distributor cooperation?

**判断自住还是项目**
- 中：方便问一下，您是自己购买使用，还是用于Airbnb、民宿、度假村或商业项目呢？不同用途适合的型号和配置会不一样。
- EN: May I know if you are buying it for personal use, or for Airbnb, homestay, resort, or a commercial project? Different usage scenarios will need different models and configurations.

**判断是不是代理商／中间商**
- 中：您是自己采购使用，还是希望在当地销售我们的房屋，做代理/经销合作呢？／您是自己需要这套房子，还是帮您的客户找产品呢？如果是帮客户找，我可以准备更适合转发的资料。
- EN: Are you purchasing for your own use, or are you interested in selling our houses locally as a distributor or dealer? / Do you need this house for yourself, or are you helping your client source the product? If you are helping a client, I can prepare more suitable materials for you to forward.

### 2. 客户问价格

**客户直接问"How much?"**
- 中：可以的。不过最终价格会根据国家、城市、型号、面积、配置和运输方式不同而变化。方便先告诉我房子准备放在哪个国家和城市吗？您是自己住，还是做民宿/项目？
- EN: Sure. The final price will depend on the country, city, model, size, configuration, and shipping method. May I know which country and city the house will be placed in? Is it for personal living or for a homestay/project?

**问"多少钱送到我这里？"**
- 中：可以帮您核算。为了查送到您地址的总成本，我需要先确认您的国家、城市和详细地址/邮编。另外想确认一下，您之前有从中国进口过大件产品吗？
- EN: Yes, I can help you calculate it. To check the total cost delivered to your address, I need to confirm your country, city, and detailed address or postal code first. Also, have you imported large products from China before?

**不直接报价的转接话术**
- 中：我可以报价，但我不想随便给您一个不准确的价格。这类房子的运费和税费影响比较大，所以我需要先确认您的位置和用途，再给您更有参考价值的价格。
- EN: I can quote, but I don't want to give you an inaccurate price casually. For this kind of house, shipping cost and tax can make a big difference, so I need to confirm your location and usage first before giving you a more useful price.

**客户不愿发详细地址**
- 中：没关系，您可以先发城市或附近区域。我先帮您做一个大概参考，后面如果需要更准确价格，再用详细地址核算。
- EN: No problem. You can send me your city or nearby area first. I can give you a rough reference first, and if you need a more accurate price later, we can calculate it with the detailed address.

**客户没有进口经验**
- 中：没关系，很多客户第一次采购这类房屋时也没有进口经验。我们可以帮您核算运输、清关、税费和派送，让您先清楚最终预算。
- EN: No problem. Many customers have no import experience when purchasing this kind of house for the first time. We can help you calculate shipping, customs clearance, tax, and local delivery, so you can understand the final budget first.

### 3. 客户嫌贵

**通用版**
- 中：我理解您的感觉。这类房子不能只看单个产品价格，还要看运输、安装、使用寿命、居住体验和后期维护成本。如果您想控制预算，我们可以根据您的用途调整型号或配置。
- EN: I understand your concern. For this kind of house, we should not only look at the product price, but also shipping, installation, service life, living experience, and later maintenance cost. If you want to control the budget, we can adjust the model or configuration based on your usage.

**终端客户说贵**
- 中：我理解。如果是个人使用，最重要的是先确认总预算能不能接受。如果这个方案超预算，我们可以帮您调整到更经济的型号，或者减少部分非必要配置。
- EN: I understand. For personal use, the most important thing is to first check whether the total budget is acceptable. If this solution is over your budget, we can adjust to a more economical model or remove some non-essential configurations.

**B端客户说贵**
- 中：我理解您的担心。不过民宿项目不能只看单套价格，还要看落地周期、出租体验、回本时间和后期复制能力。如果您希望降低第一阶段投入，可以先从样板房或少量房间开始。
- EN: I understand your concern. But for a homestay project, we should not only look at the unit price, but also the landing timeline, guest experience, payback period, and later replication ability. If you want to reduce the first-stage investment, you can start with a sample unit or a small number of rooms first.

**运费贵**
- 中：是的，这类房子体积比较大，所以运费会占比较高。如果您有自己的货代，也可以让他们核算运输成本，可能会更低。我们也可以继续帮您找更合适的运输方案。
- EN: Yes, because this kind of house has a large volume, the shipping cost can be a big part of the total cost. If you have your own freight forwarder, you can ask them to calculate the shipping cost, and it may be lower. We can also continue helping you find a more suitable shipping solution.

### 4. 要求打折／要最低价

（核心策略见"金句库·三、四"；卡在价格时先拆分报价：房屋基础价／家具／运费税费／安装支持／可选升级，让客户知道贵在哪里，再决定让价、拆配置还是调整付款。）

**争取现场/首单支持（客户愿当天确认时）**
- 中：如果您今天能确认样板/首批订单，我可以现在帮您去跟老板申请一次现场支持。但我不想通过降低质量来做低价格，我们可以看哪些非核心配置可以调整，哪些地方可以给您一些首单支持。
- EN: If you can confirm the sample or first batch order today, I can go to my boss now and apply for special on-site support for you. But I don't want to reduce the price by reducing quality. We can check which non-essential parts can be adjusted and where we can give you first-order support.

### 5. 质疑质量／怕被骗

**建立信任**
- 中：我理解您的担心，跨国采购房屋确实需要谨慎。所以我们建议您先通过视频看真实样板房、材料细节和工厂情况。您也可以看我们的项目案例，再决定是否继续推进。
- EN: I understand your concern. Overseas house purchasing does need to be careful. That's why we suggest you first check the real sample house, material details, and factory situation by video. You can also check our project cases before deciding whether to move forward.

**客户想看工厂**
- 中：可以的。我们可以安排视频看展厅或工厂。如果您后面来中国，也可以安排现场参观。
- EN: Yes, no problem. We can arrange a video showroom or factory visit. If you come to China later, we can also arrange an on-site visit.

### 6. 担心安装

**普通解释**
- 中：安装问题您不用太担心。我们可以提供安装图纸、安装视频和线上指导。如果项目数量比较多，也可以根据实际情况评估是否安排工程师或安装团队支持。
- EN: You don't need to worry too much about installation. We can provide installation drawings, installation videos, and online guidance. If the project quantity is large, we can also evaluate whether to arrange an engineer or installation team to support it.

**没有安装团队**
- 中：如果您当地暂时没有安装团队，我们可以先根据项目数量和位置评估安装支持方式。有些项目可以通过当地工人加线上指导完成，有些项目可以考虑工程师指导。
- EN: If you don't have a local installation team for now, we can first evaluate the installation support method based on the project quantity and location. Some projects can be completed by local workers with online guidance, and some projects can consider engineer guidance.

### 7. 客户说"还要考虑"

**普通版**
- 中：没问题，您可以先考虑。方便问一下，您现在主要是在考虑价格、运输、安装，还是产品是否适合您的场地？我可以根据您的顾虑，给您补充更有用的信息。
- EN: No problem, you can think about it first. May I know whether you are mainly considering the price, shipping, installation, or whether the product is suitable for your site? I can provide more useful information based on your concern.

**推进下一步版**
- 中：您可以先和家人/合伙人讨论。为了方便您判断，我可以整理一份图片、视频和基础资料给您。您看完以后，我们再确认下一步是否需要核算落地成本。
- EN: You can discuss it with your family/partners first. To help you judge, I can organize pictures, videos, and basic materials for you. After you check them, we can confirm whether we need to calculate the landed cost next.

### 8. 报价后跟进／客户不回复激活

**发案例激活**
- 中：您好，最近我们有一个新的房屋案例，比较适合私人土地、民宿和度假项目。我发您参考一下，如果您后面还在考虑这个项目，我可以继续帮您核算方案。
- EN: Hi, we recently have a new house case, which is suitable for private land, homestay, and vacation projects. I am sending it to you for reference. If you are still considering this project later, I can continue helping you calculate the solution.

**问项目进度**
- 中：您好，之前您咨询过我们的房屋。想问一下您现在项目进展到哪一步了？是还在选款、做预算，还是已经开始准备场地了？
- EN: Hi, you asked about our house before. May I know what stage your project is at now? Are you still selecting models and making the budget, or have you started preparing the site?

**更新价格/运费激活**
- 中：您好，之前您了解过这款房子。如果您现在还在考虑，我可以帮您重新更新一下当前价格和运输成本。
- EN: Hi, you checked this house before. If you are still considering it, I can help you update the current price and shipping cost.

**轻激活**
- 中：您好，最近您这个项目还有继续推进吗？如果需要，我可以继续帮您看适合的型号和方案。
- EN: Hi, is your project still moving forward recently? If needed, I can continue helping you check suitable models and solutions.

### 9. 客户突然回来

**客户问"还在吗？"**
- 中：在的。您现在是准备重新推进这个房屋计划了吗？我可以先帮您更新一下当前资料和成本参考。
- EN: Yes, I'm here. Are you planning to move forward with this house plan again? I can first help you update the current materials and cost reference.

**重新问价格**
- 中：可以，我帮您更新。您之前的地址还是【地址】吗？如果地址或需求有变化，我可以重新核算更准确的价格。
- EN: Sure, I can update it for you. Is your previous address still 【address】? If the address or requirements have changed, I can recalculate a more accurate price.

### 10. 客户比价／说别人更便宜

**不只比空房价格**
- 中：可以理解，项目采购多比较是正常的。不过建议您不要只比较空房价格，还要确认是否包含运输、税费、清关、派送、基础配置、安装支持和售后资料。有时候最低报价不代表最终落地成本最低。
- EN: I understand. It is normal to compare suppliers for a project purchase. But I suggest you don't only compare the empty house price. You also need to check whether shipping, tax, customs clearance, local delivery, basic configuration, installation support, and after-sales documents are included. Sometimes the lowest quotation does not mean the lowest final landed cost.

**别人更便宜**
- 中：明白。不同供应商的配置、材料、运输方式和包含内容可能不一样。您可以把对方包含的内容对比一下，如果只是空房价格低，最终落地成本可能不一定低。
- EN: I understand. Different suppliers may have different configurations, materials, shipping methods, and included items. You can compare what is included. If it is only a lower empty house price, the final landed cost may not necessarily be lower.

### 11. 代理商场景

**第一轮筛选**
- 中：很高兴了解您的合作意向。为了判断是否适合建立代理合作，方便先了解一下您的公司情况吗？您现在主要销售什么产品？有没有公司、展厅、安装团队或当地客户资源？
- EN: Glad to know your cooperation interest. To see whether we are suitable for a distributor partnership, may I first understand your company situation? What products are you mainly selling now? Do you have a company, showroom, installation team, or local customer resources?

**要独家代理**
- 中：独家代理可以作为后续合作方向，但我们不会一开始直接确认。通常我们会先通过样品、展厅展示和实际销售结果来验证当地市场能力。如果后续销售结果达到双方目标，我们可以进一步讨论区域保护或独家合作。
- EN: Exclusive agency can be a future cooperation direction, but we cannot confirm it at the beginning. Normally, we first verify the local market capability through samples, showroom display, and actual sales results. If the later sales results meet both sides' targets, we can further discuss regional protection or exclusive cooperation.

**要免费样品**
- 中：房屋产品和普通小商品不同，样品本身成本、运输成本和安装成本都比较高，所以我们一般不提供免费样品。如果您认真做当地代理，建议先采购样品用于展厅展示或客户体验。
- EN: House products are different from small products. The sample cost, shipping cost, and installation cost are all relatively high, so we generally do not provide free samples. If you seriously want to become a local distributor, we suggest purchasing sample units first for showroom display or customer experience.

### 12. 中间商场景

**客户保护**
- 中：您放心，只要是您介绍的客户，我们都会保护好您的客户关系和利益，不会绕过您去直接抢客户。如果后面需要我们协助视频介绍、看展厅或接待客户，我们也会提前和您沟通。
- EN: Please don't worry. For any client introduced by you, we will protect your client relationship and your interest. We will not bypass you to take the client directly. If you need us to help with video introduction, showroom viewing, or customer reception later, we will communicate with you in advance.

**返点/利润保护**
- 中：我们公司支持中间人合作。如果您有真实客户推荐给我们，我们会给您预留合理的利润或返点空间。具体方式可以根据项目金额、客户情况和您的参与程度来沟通。
- EN: Our company supports cooperation with middlemen and sourcing partners. If you introduce real clients to us, we will reserve reasonable profit or commission space for you. The exact method can be discussed based on the project amount, client situation, and your level of involvement.

**无logo资料**
- 中：如果您需要转发给客户，我们可以给您准备不带logo的图片、视频和产品资料。报价单也可以做成PDF或Excel版本，方便您转发或自行调整。
- EN: If you need to forward the materials to your client, we can prepare pictures, videos, and product documents without our logo. The quotation can also be prepared in PDF or Excel format according to your needs.

### 13. 视频邀约与视频后推进

**通用视频邀约**
- 中：如果您方便的话，我们可以约一个视频电话。我可以带您线上看真实样板房，包括外观、内部空间、材料、墙面、地板、卫生间和厨房细节。这样您会更直观地了解产品。
- EN: If you are available, we can schedule a video call. I can show you the real sample house online, including the exterior, interior space, materials, wall panels, flooring, bathroom, and kitchen details. This will help you understand the product more directly.

**视频后总结推进**
- 中：刚刚视频里我们主要看了外观、内部空间、材料、卫生间、厨房和安装细节。根据您的需求，我觉得下一步可以先确认【数量/场地/配置/运输/报价】。您方便先把【资料】发我吗？我来继续帮您推进。
- EN: In the video just now, we mainly checked the exterior, interior space, materials, bathroom, kitchen, and installation details. Based on your needs, I think the next step is to confirm the 【quantity/site/configuration/shipping/quotation】 first. Could you send me the 【information】? I will continue helping you move forward.

### 14. 合同/PI/付款

**客户要合同**
- 中：可以的。在出正式合同/PI前，我们需要先确认最终型号、数量、配置、收货信息、运输方式和付款条款。确认后我们可以给您准备正式文件。
- EN: Yes, no problem. Before issuing the official contract/PI, we need to confirm the final model, quantity, configuration, receiving information, shipping method, and payment terms. After confirmation, we can prepare the official document for you.

**问付款方式**
- 中：通常我们会根据订单金额和项目情况确认付款方式。如果您已经确认型号和数量，我可以帮您向公司申请适合这个订单的付款条款。
- EN: Normally, the payment terms will be confirmed based on the order amount and project situation. If you have confirmed the model and quantity, I can help apply for suitable payment terms for this order from our company.

### 15. 信息收集后总结（结束对话）

- 中：好的，我先总结一下您的需求：房子放在【国家/城市】，用途是【用途】，计划【数量】套，大概需要【面积/户型】，目前主要关注【卡点】。下一步我会帮您【动作】，您这边可以先提供【资料】。
- EN: Okay, let me summarize your needs first: The house will be placed in 【country/city】, the usage is 【usage】, the planned quantity is 【quantity】 units, the approximate size/layout is 【size/layout】, and your main concern now is 【concern】. Next, I will help you 【action】, and you can first provide 【information】.

### 16. 销售慎用话术（禁忌）

- "This is our best price."——除非真到底价，否则不说。
- "No problem, we can do everything."——不乱承诺，确认后再说。
- "We can ship to your home for sure."——没查清楚前不说死。
- "Free sample is okay."——房屋类不承诺免费样品。
- "You can be our exclusive agent."——不能一开始承诺独家。
- "The installation is very easy."——安装要讲清地基、水电、吊装和当地团队。
- "Don't worry."——客户担心时要给具体解决方案，不是一句别担心。

## 二、高信任成交金句库（老板型销售/大客户/项目客户）

### A. 把自己变成"负责项目的人"

1. EN: My friend, for me this is not just about selling you a house. I want to make sure your whole project goes smoothly from the first discussion to final installation. If the house arrives but you cannot install it properly, or if the total cost is not clear, then even a good price means nothing. So I will help you check everything step by step and make sure you feel safe before moving forward.
中：不是简单卖你一套房，我会一步步帮你确认清楚，让你放心再往前走。

2. EN: I don't want to win this order by giving you a beautiful promise. I want to win your trust by helping you avoid mistakes before they happen. For this kind of house, many problems don't appear before payment; they appear during shipping, customs clearance, installation, and local preparation. That is why I ask many details now. It is not to make things complicated. It is to protect your project.
中：我问很多细节不是把事情变复杂，是为了保护你的项目。

3. EN: If you only need a cheap quotation, many suppliers can give it to you. But if you need someone to help you think about the real landing process, shipping risk, installation, cost control, and future use, then I'm willing to spend more time with you. Because I don't want this to be only one transaction. I want this to be a successful project for you and a long-term relationship for us.
中：便宜报价很多人能给；我要的是你项目成功和长期合作。

### B. 拉近距离："我在你这边"

4. EN: My friend, I know you want the best price, and I also know you are not just bargaining for fun. You are trying to control your risk and make sure this decision is worth it. So I will not play games with you. I will check what I can fight for, and I will tell you honestly what can change and what cannot change.
中：你不是为砍价而砍价，是在控制风险。我不玩套路，哪些能争取、哪些不能改，我诚实告诉你。

5. EN: I understand your position. If I were buying from another country, I would also ask many questions, compare prices, and worry about shipping, installation, and after-sales. So please feel free to ask me anything. My job is not only to answer you, but to help you feel clear and confident before you make the decision.
中：换位理解，我的工作是让你决定前足够清楚、有信心。

6. EN: You don't need to trust me only because I say we are good. I will show you real videos, real cases, real materials, and real project details. You can judge by yourself. Trust should not come from words only; it should come from facts.
中：信任不该只来自语言，应来自事实，你自己判断。

### C. 价格谈判

7. EN: I just spoke with my boss again. I pushed for the best support I could get for your order, because I believe this first cooperation is more important than short-term profit. The final price we can offer is 【price】. Honestly, there is not much room left, but I will make sure you get the quality and support you deserve.
中：刚又跟老板争取了，最终价【价格】，空间不多，但质量和支持我保证。

8. EN: I don't want to reduce the price by reducing quality. That would be irresponsible to your project and also bad for our long-term cooperation. ## 三、客户心理与信任故事库

**故事1｜客户问"How much?"**
适用：客户开场只问价。梗概：问价背后是在问"买不买得起、有没有隐藏费用、安不安全、能不能落地"，直接甩数字只会引出更多疑问。
关键话术：I can give you the price, but I don't want to give you a cheap-looking number that cannot really land later. The real key is not only the factory price. We need to check the shipping, customs clearance, tax, installation... If you tell me your country, city, and usage, I can help you judge the real budget more clearly.

**故事2｜有些问价只是询价**
适用：客户只问价不给任何信息。梗概：国家、城市、用途、数量、时间全不答的，大概率是弱询价客户——留着、备注、低频发案例，不当重点。值得重跟的客户一定暴露过真实信息（有土地/做民宿/城市/套数/审批/合伙人/来中国/要样板）。
关键点：客户问价只是入口，给信息才是质量，愿意推进才是机会。

**故事3｜客户说"best price"**
适用：客户要最低价。梗概：多数不是压死你，而是在控制风险、测试你是否上心；先说"我不玩价格游戏"，再真的去"跟老板争取一次"。
关键话术：见金句4、7。

**故事4｜客户提付款条件**
适用：客户提出分段付款（如30/30/20/20）。梗概：这是订单风险分配信号，说明他当真了；不说"No, impossible"，也不乱答应。
关键话术：I understand why you want to arrange the payment this way. You are not refusing to pay; you just want every step of the project to feel safer... Let's see which parts can be adjusted and which parts are company and industry rules that cannot be changed. My goal is not to put all the risk on you, but to find a way that is safe for both sides.

**故事5｜客户问"你们从哪里发货"**
适用：客户问产地/工厂。梗概：他在确认你是真是假、是不是中间商；借机建立工厂背书而非只答"from China"。
关键话术：We ship from China. We have several production bases in China, and different factories focus on different types of houses. This allows us to match the right production and delivery solution based on the product you choose, your destination, and your project needs.

**故事6｜客户说"准备下单"**
适用：客户释放成交信号。梗概：不催付款，先接住信任、承诺负责，再确认信息、推进PI。
关键话术：Thank you for your trust. From now on, I will treat this as my own project to follow. Before we prepare the PI, let me confirm the final quantity, company information, payment terms, and delivery details, so we can avoid mistakes in the contract.

**故事7｜客户说"我再考虑一下"**
适用：客户拖延。梗概：不回"Okay, waiting for your reply"；帮他拆出真正卡点（价格/运费/合伙人/安装/审批/怕被骗/比价）。
关键话术：Of course, you can think about it... Usually when customers say they need to think, they are stuck on one or two key concerns. Is your main concern now the price, shipping, installation, quality, or family/partner decision?

**故事8｜买家具的故事：不回≠不要**
适用：客户不回复后的心态判断。梗概：真实买家也会因为忙、出差、等合伙人/土地/资金而中断，需求还在只是时间窗口关了。激活不是每天问"Are you still interested?"（那是骚扰），而是刷高质量存在感，等窗口重开时你还在他视野里。客户突然回来时窗口可能只有几分钟——必须秒回，重点客户置顶标星。

**故事9｜不是所有客户都值得激活**
适用：分配跟进精力。梗概：判断标准不是问没问过价，而是给没给过有效信息（国家城市/用途/土地/型号/场地/时间/决策人）。只会"How much / Send catalog / Best price"的，低频沉淀即可。

**故事10｜八种"不回"的处置**
适用：客户不回后的分类决策。梗概：①只询价不给信息→低频沉淀；②问过价无地址用途数量→弱意向偶发案例；③有用途无土地时间→养着；④有土地有项目有数量但卡价格/运费/审批/安装→重点激活；⑤久不回突然回来→快速响应；⑥每次只回一点但没消失→约电话一次问清；⑦明确接受不了价格且无替代空间→低频沉淀；⑧只要目录最低价资料、关键问题全不答→看市场的，不重点跟。

**故事11｜激活要给回复理由**
适用：二次激活。梗概：发相似案例、新户型、实拍视频、运输交付案例、他关心问题的解决方案，而不是"Any update?"。前者提供价值，后者索取回复。
关键话术：见金句31、33。

**故事12｜客户突然回来**
适用：沉默数周后客户回来问价/问货。梗概：他可能重新进入决策期、比完价、资金到位；不要当新客户重新介绍，直接更新关键信息带回决策，同时要快——他也在找别人。
关键话术：见金句34。

**故事13｜B端客户是在做投资决策**
适用：民宿/度假村/营地项目客户。梗概：他想的是能否按期开业、客人满意度、日租、回本、复制、审批、安装拖不拖；把表达从"卖房"升级为"项目顾问"。
关键话术：This is not simply buying several houses. You are making an accommodation project investment... we cannot only look at the unit price. We need to calculate the site, quantity, shipping, installation, and opening timeline together.

**故事14｜有土地的B端客户要快推**
适用：客户说"I already have land"。梗概：有无土地是第一分水岭；有土地立刻提优先级，快速确认场地、能放几套、启动开业时间、安装团队、预算。
关键话术：If the land is already confirmed, this project should not stay only at the picture-checking stage anymore. The next step should be to quickly judge: how many units can be placed on the site, which model is most suitable, how shipping should be arranged, who will handle installation, and whether the overall budget works.

**故事15｜没土地的客户不做深度方案**
适用：客户说要做度假村/买很多套但土地未定。梗概：先给产品和预算范围养着，土地确定后再快速做准确规划，保护销售时间。
关键话术：If the land is not confirmed yet, I don't suggest making a very detailed solution now. Because the quantity, placement, shipping method, foundation, and installation plan will all depend on the site.

**故事16｜一直要资料可能是拖延决策**
适用：客户不停要目录/视频/图片但不动。梗概：他可能没进采购阶段，资料越发越乱；发资料后拉回关键问题。
关键话术：I can keep sending more materials, but I don't want your project to stay forever at the "checking information" stage. More pictures will not move the project forward. The real key is to confirm a few things: where the house will be placed, what it will be used for, how many units, when, and whether your biggest concern is price or installation.

**故事17｜客户说别人更便宜**
适用：比价压价。梗概：他在测试你的价值和底线，不要立刻跟降；先重新定义比较标准。
关键话术：It is right for you to compare different suppliers. But I suggest you don't only compare the empty house price. What really needs to be compared is the complete landing solution: whether the materials are the same, whether the configuration is clear, whether shipping and tax are calculated, who will support installation, and who will be responsible if problems happen later.

**故事18｜代理商开口就要政策/独家**
适用："I want to be your agent / exclusive agency"。梗概：不马上发政策底价；看他有无公司、渠道、展厅、安装团队、样板计划、来中国意愿。原则：先样板后政策，先付款后独家，先证明实力后给支持。
关键话术：If you seriously want to build the local market, I suggest starting with a sample first, not discussing exclusivity at the beginning. Exclusive agency is discussed later based on market investment and sales results, not decided by verbal interest.

**故事19｜中间商最怕被抢客户**
适用：中间商询盘。梗概：第一件事不是报价，是让他放心你不绕单；他敢不敢推客户取决于信不信你讲规矩。
关键话术：见金句26、27。

**故事20｜视频电话不是看房**
适用：视频会议前后。梗概：有效视频要完成四件事：看真实产品、判断适配场地、问出文字问不出的信息、确认下一步动作；结束前必须总结并布置下一步。
关键话术：Today we checked the exterior, interior space, materials, bathroom, kitchen, and installation details. Based on your needs, the next most important step is to confirm the site and quantity. Please send me the site pictures or location...

**故事21｜别说"Trust me"**
适用：客户不信任。梗概："Trust me / Don't worry / We are professional"说多了反而不信；让客户用事实自己判断。
关键话术：见金句6。

**故事22｜付款后信任才刚开始**
适用：成交后。梗概：客户付款后更焦虑（开始生产没有、会不会延期、到港怎么办）；销售不能消失，第一句话要接住信任。
关键话术：见金句36、37。

**故事库总纲**：客户不是被话术打动的，是被"你理解他的风险"打动的。高手表达=我知道你担心什么＋我知道项目会卡在哪＋我不给你落不了地的低价方案＋我帮你拆清运输/清关/安装/场地/时间/预算＋不用现在做最终决定，先确认一个关键点＋项目能跑通我陪你走到落地。

## 四、其他关键销售技巧（可操作规则）

**印度客户/现场客户**
- 不一开始报死价，必须留空间：价格、配置、家具、付款条件、现场成交空间各留一刀。
- 顺序：先正常报价→看是否认真→看是否来中国→看有无明确数量→再决定让多少。
- 客户已在中国或明确近期来：不只接待发资料，必须带成交动作——现场确认需求、数量、预算、背后终端客户、样板单、付款条件，现场争取老板支持，推动PI或定金。他既然来了就不是普通询价。
- 中间人带客户来：同时处理两层关系，提前向中间人承诺保护客户关系、不绕单、价格利润提前谈清。

**基础价＋带家具价组合报价**
- 只报全包价客户嫌贵；只报空房价后面家具/空调/热水器/安装全另算，总成本不低。组合报价让客户自己判断。
- 基础价=测预算，适合初询、预算不明、价格敏感、中间商转发；带家具价=拉价值，适合自住、度假、民宿、要快速入住开业的客户。
- 标准表达：中：我给您两个预算方向，不是让您一定选贵的，而是让您清楚差别。方案一是基础房屋价格，适合先控制预算，后期家具家电当地自己配。方案二是带家具/基础可入住方案，适合想省时间、快速入住或快速做民宿上线的客户。EN: I can give you two budget directions... Option 1 is the basic house price, suitable if you want to control the budget and prepare furniture locally later. Option 2 is the furnished / ready-to-use solution, suitable if you want to save time, move in faster, or launch a homestay project quickly.
- 家具、阳台、橱柜、门窗升级不要一开始送完，留作后续谈判空间。
- 客户卡价格时先拆分（房屋/家具/运费税费/安装/升级），让他知道贵在哪，再决定让价还是拆配置。

**报价策略：前期快，后期准**
- 报价三目的：测预算、测真实意向、推下一步动作。报价后必须观察：还回不回、问不问细节/运费/安装、愿不愿视频、给不给地址场地数量。
- 前期用聊天报价（1户型图＋1实拍视频＋1参考价＋1包含说明＋1下一步问题），不做复杂quotation；深度沟通后正式报价必须确认型号、户型、数量、配置、目的地、是否含运/安装/家具等。
- 不报最高（客户跑），不报最低（没空间）；基础参考价是测预算，不是正式quotation。

**复盘规则**
- 每个重点客户每天答五问：客户哪一类？真实需求？拿到了哪些关键信息？当前卡点？下一步具体动作？答不上=没跟明白。
- 不写虚词（"有意向/再考虑/不回/觉得贵"），要写具体卡点：卡在运费/清关/安装/合伙人决策/审批/家人/价格超预算/总落地成本不清/没土地/没本地安装团队。
- 日报最重要的不是"我跟了"，而是"我推进了什么"。

## 五、销售全流程与核心原则（总览提炼）

**全流程阶段**：接询盘（15分钟内响应）→ 判断客户身份（代理商/建筑商/民宿营地/个人/华人/中间人）→ 需求挖掘（固定顺序十问：国家城市→身份→用途→数量→采购时间→土地项目→预算→进口经验→安装团队→认证要求）→ 客户分级 A/B/C/D → 产品推荐（按客户类型匹配）→ 报价与解释（价格/配置/运费/总成本/付款）→ 异议处理（贵/运费/进口/安装/认证/信任）→ 逼单推进（视频会议/样板单/定金）→ 按等级节奏跟进与激活 → 成交/丢单复盘。

**成交信号（出现即推进）**：问付款方式→推定金；问交期→有采购时间；问运输装柜→进入落地阶段；问合同→强信号；问优惠→进入价格谈判；问安装细节/发土地图→项目真实度提高；问样板、愿意视频、让你重新报价→立即重点跟进。

**核心原则**：销售不是客服，是引导客户成交；客户买的不是房子——代理商买复购、民宿买回报、个人买安全感；客户问价不直接报价，先问国家、数量、用途、配置、运输；客户说贵先判断是预算、配置还是信任问题；红线：不乱承诺、不乱报价、不飞单、不私藏客户。
